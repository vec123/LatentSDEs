Dynamical Systems as Basis Expansions

We approximate drift f(x) as ∑ φi(x) θi.

System Required Basis Functions Complexity
Linear (OU) {x1, x2} Low
Double-Well {x1, x2, x13, x23} Medium
Van der Pol {x1, x2, x12x2} High (Non-linear coupling)

Mathematical Formulation

For the Van der Pol oscillator, the drift f(x) is mapped as:

  • f1(x) = 1 · x2
  • f2(x) = μ · x2 - μ · (x12x2) - 1 · x1
where we choose μ = 1.0. This illustrates how even simple polynomial basis functions can capture complex dynamics when combined appropriately.

This demonstrates how non-linear dynamics emerge from simple linear combinations of expanded state-space features.
